Questions

Good to know.

Where does the data come from?

The Dubai Land Department's open data (via Dubai Data), refreshed daily for projects and weekly for market statistics, plus developers' official launch details for brand-new projects.

What counts as one project?

Each different project you run a report on in a month. Send the same project to 10 clients and it still counts once.

Will my clients see someone else's name?

No. The report carries your name, photo, agency, BRN and WhatsApp. Your clients contact you.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. It works in any browser, phone or laptop. Log in with your email and password.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. After you verify your email you can run 3 different projects free. Trial reports show Sam Surya's name and number with a FREE TRIAL watermark. Subscribe to remove it and brand every report with your own details.

How do I pay and when does it start?

Pay by card or Apple Pay with Ziina, send the receipt on WhatsApp, and your plan is activated the same day for one month.

Can I change my details later?

Yes. Tap ✏️ Edit my details in your dashboard any time.
