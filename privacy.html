Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

About this application

Contents for Real Estate is a personal application used to manage and publish real estate educational videos to the owner's YouTube channel.

Google and YouTube data

The application uses Google OAuth authorization to access the owner's YouTube account for authorized video uploads and related publishing operations.

Data storage and security

Authorization credentials are stored privately and are not published on this website. Access is limited to authorized application operations.

Data sharing

The application does not sell personal information. Data is shared with Google and YouTube as necessary to perform authorized publishing operations.

Revoking access

Google account access can be revoked through Google Account permissions .
