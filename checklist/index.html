🛡️ Is it safe?

Dubai protects off-plan buyers well. Make sure you're inside that protection.

A developer registered with RERA is accountable to the regulator, and their delivery record is the best guide to whether your building gets finished on time. 🏗️3–4

The exact project is registered, not just the developer

A good developer isn't enough. Every off-plan project needs its own DLD registration before it can legally be sold, and without it your payments and your unit aren't protected. Pick your project at the top and we pull its registration, status and construction progress from DLD records for you. Not registered yet? Step 2 of 3 · Checks 5–9

🔐 Is your money protected?

Where your money goes and who you deal with.

🏦5–7

Your money goes into the project's escrow account

By law (Law No. 8 of 2007), off-plan payments go into a regulated escrow account for that project, not the developer's company account. When your project is registered, we show you its escrow bank from DLD records, so the only thing left for you is to check that the account on your payment instructions matches it.

⚠️ Never pay a deposit into a personal or general company account.
🪪8–9

Your agent is licensed

Only a RERA-licensed agent can legally sell you property, and a licensed agent is answerable to the regulator if something goes wrong. This is the one thing we can't look up for you: ask your agent for their BRN (Broker Registration Number), and make sure the agency and the agent are named on your paperwork.

Step 2 of 3 · Checks 11–14

📈 Is it right for you?

It has to fit your cash flow and your contract.

📅11–12

The payment plan fits your cash flow

Most buyers who get into trouble didn't pick a bad project; they ran short of cash between milestones. Write down every payment: how much and when, whether it's tied to dates or to construction progress, and whether there's a post-handover plan. Add it in the calculator below and we work out how much you pay before the keys.

📝13–14

The SPA (Sale & Purchase Agreement) is clear

The SPA is what the developer legally owes you; the brochure isn't. Make sure it states the unit number, size, floor plan, finishes, payment schedule, the completion date and what happens if handover is late. Add your SPA handover date below and we compare it with the completion date the developer registered with DLD.

Step 2 of 3 · Checks 15–17

⚖️ Lock in your rights

Registration and the fine print.

📜15

Your purchase is registered (Oqood)

Oqood is DLD's interim registration of your off-plan purchase. It records the unit in your name during construction, protects your rights if anything goes wrong, and becomes your title deed at handover. Ask the developer to confirm in writing that it will be issued, and when.

⚖️16–17

You know the risk if you stop paying

If a buyer defaults, Dubai rules let the developer keep part of what was paid, depending on construction progress. Know the terms before you sign.
