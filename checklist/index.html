Checks 1–2

🛡️ Is it safe?

Dubai protects off-plan buyers well. Make sure you're inside that protection.

🏢01

The developer is registered with RERA

Open the official Dubai REST app (Dubai Land Department) and search the developer. Look at their completed projects and on-time handovers.

🏗️02

The exact project is registered, not just the developer

Every off-plan project has its own registration. Ask for the project number and search it on Dubai REST to see its status and construction progress.

Checks 3–4

🔐 Protect your money

Where your money goes and who you deal with.

🏦03

Your money goes into the project's escrow account

By law (Law No. 8 of 2007), off-plan payments go into a regulated escrow account for that project, not the developer's company account.

⚠️ Never pay a deposit into a personal or general company account.
🪪04

Your agent is licensed

Ask for the agent's BRN (Broker Registration Number) and verify it on Dubai REST.

Check 5

💰 Know the real cost

The price is not the whole story.

💰05

You know the full cost, not just the price

DLD ~4%
Deposit 10–20%
Handover

Oqood is usually paid at booking (some launches cover or share it, so ask). At handover budget for DEWA, cooling (if any) and service charges. Get any agency fee in writing.

📱TIP

Your #1 free tool: the Dubai REST app

Free on the App Store and Google Play, from the Dubai Land Department. Checks 1 to 4 take about 10 minutes in the app. If a project or agent isn't there, walk away.

Checks 6–7

📈 Is it right for you?

It has to fit your cash flow and your contract.

📅06

The payment plan fits your cash flow

Write down every milestone: how much and when. Is it linked to dates or to construction progress? Is there a post-handover plan?

📝07

The SPA (Sale & Purchase Agreement) is clear

Check unit number, size, floor plan, finishes, payment schedule, the expected completion date and what happens if handover is late.

Checks 8–9

⚖️ Lock in your rights

Registration and the fine print.

📜08

Your purchase is registered (Oqood)

Oqood is the DLD's interim registration of your off-plan purchase. It protects your rights during construction and becomes your title deed at handover.

⚖️09

You know the risk if you stop paying

If a buyer defaults, Dubai rules let the developer keep part of what was paid, depending on construction progress. Know the terms before you sign.

Check 10

🧭 Plan your way out

Know your exit before you commit.

🚪10

Your exit plan makes sense

🏠 Live · 🔑 Rent: compare rents for similar ready units · 💸 Sell: check real resale prices on DLD data.

🌟 Golden Visa: property worth AED 2M+ can qualify for the 10-year UAE Golden Visa, off-plan included. Confirm current DLD conditions for your case.
🔐RULE

The golden rule

No Dubai REST listing, no escrow account, no Oqood? No deal. A good project passes all 10 checks easily.
