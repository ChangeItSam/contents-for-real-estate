How do I verify a real estate agent in Dubai?

Ask for the agent's Broker Registration Number (BRN). Check it in the Dubai Land Department's Dubai REST app under Services → Licensed Real Estate Brokers, or on the DLD licence and permit verification page. You'll see the broker's office and broker card. My BRN is 58987.

Source: Dubai Land Department, Dubai REST app guide

What fees do I pay when buying off-plan in Dubai?

The main cost is the Dubai Land Department's 4% registration fee on the property value. It's usually paid when you sign the Sales and Purchase Agreement, at the point the sale is registered on Oqood, DLD's off-plan register. Some developers waive all or part of it as a promotion. That's a developer offer, not a government rule, and it shouldn't be your only reason to pick a project.

Source: Dubai Land Department

Can I get a UAE Golden Visa with an off-plan property?

Yes. The 10-year Golden Visa through property needs a DLD value of at least AED 2 million. Off-plan and mortgaged properties can count, and there's no longer a minimum amount you must have paid. Check the current conditions with the DLD or ICP before you apply.

Source: Dubai Land Department / ICP guidance, 2026

How is my money protected when I buy off-plan in Dubai?

Your payments go into the project's escrow account, and the developer can draw on it only as construction progresses. Pay only into the escrow account named in your SPA, never a personal account. Before you book, check the project's registration and completion percentage in the Dubai REST app.

Source: Dubai Land Department, Real Estate Brokerage Practice Guide

How do I know if an off-plan project is worth buying?

Compare five things: the developer's delivery record, the price per sq ft against the area, the payment plan against your cash flow, upcoming supply nearby, and realistic rent after handover. My free Off-Plan Stress Test runs 20 of these checks and tells you whether to buy or dig deeper.
