FAQ

Questions

What counts as one project on Agent Pro?

One project you run a full report for. Re-opening or sharing the same project's report again in the same month doesn't use another one.

What if I run out of my 30 projects?

Top up any time for AED 19 per extra project, or move to Agent Unlimited for AED 299 a month.

Where does the data come from?

Dubai Land Department open data published on Dubai Data (CC BY 4.0): project registrations, escrow, construction progress and transactions. Market figures update every week.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Plans are month to month. Message me on WhatsApp before your next payment and it stops.

Is this financial advice?

No. It's a data check to help you ask the right questions. Always read your SPA and confirm with the developer.
