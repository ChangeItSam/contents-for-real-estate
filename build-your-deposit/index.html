How the numbers workSimple, visible maths: change any box above

Cash up front = price × first payment % + price × DLD fee %. Off-plan payment plans often start at 10-20% with the rest paid during construction and at handover; each project is different, so check the developer's plan.

DLD fee: Dubai Land Department registration is 4% of the price, plus small admin fees (dubailand.gov.ae). Some launches pay part of it for you: that is per developer, subject to availability.

Months to save = what's left ÷ what you save per month. Extra income needed = what's left ÷ months you chose − what you already save.
