🇦🇪 10-year Golden Visa

Your Golden Visa through a Dubai home

Buy property worth AED 2 million or more, off-plan included, and you can apply for the 10-year renewable UAE Golden Visa through the Dubai Land Department. Here's exactly how it works.

Do you qualify?

What counts

  • Purchase value of AED 2 million or more, in one property or several together.
  • Off-plan units from approved developers count at their full contract (Oqood) value, even before you've paid it all.
  • Mortgaged homes qualify too: you add a bank no-objection letter showing the amount paid and the balance.
  • Family: you can sponsor your spouse, children and parents.
  • Joint ownership: each owner's share must be at least AED 2 million.
  • The value is the purchase value certified by the Dubai Land Department, not an estimate or the price you hope to resell at.
  • You must be inside the UAE when you apply, and you attend in person.

How to apply

  1. Buy and register the property (Oqood for off-plan, title deed for ready) at AED 2M+.
  2. Prepare: passport, title deed or Oqood/e-title, personal photo, Emirates ID and current visa if you have them, plus the bank NOC if mortgaged.
  3. Visit a DLD service centre, submit and pay the fees.
  4. Medical test at the centre.
  5. Receive your residence permit by email: DLD says 7 to 10 business days.

Questions people ask me

I'm buying off-plan. Can I apply before handover?

Yes. Off-plan from an approved developer counts at the full Oqood value, so you can apply once the sale is registered with DLD. Ask the developer for the documents DLD needs (Oqood, statement of account) before you pay the booking.

Can I combine two cheaper units?

Yes: several properties can be combined as long as together they reach AED 2 million in your name.

My budget is under AED 2M. Is there another visa?

There's the 2-year property investor visa. In 2026 DLD changed its rules (reported in May 2026 as removing the old AED 750,000 minimum for sole owners), but it applies to completed properties with a title deed, not off-plan. Rules change often, so check the DLD investor visa service or message me and I'll confirm the current position.

Does the visa need me to live in the UAE?

The Golden Visa doesn't carry the usual 6-month rule for staying abroad, which is one of its main advantages. Confirm your own case with ICP/GDRFA.
