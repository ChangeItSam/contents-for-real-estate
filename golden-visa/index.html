Get a 10-year UAE Golden Visa through Dubai property

Buy property worth AED 2 million or more and you can apply for a 10-year renewable residence visa for you and your family. Here are the rules in plain English, then a finder for projects where units have sold from AED 2M.

  • AED 2M purchase value, wholly owned in your name. DLD ›
  • One or several properties can be combined to reach AED 2M. GDRFA ›
  • Joint owners: your own share must be worth AED 2M. GDRFA ›
  • Off-plan can count when bought from a developer approved by the authority. MoET ›
  • Mortgaged is possible with a bank no-objection letter showing the amount paid. DLD ›
  • Family: spouse, children and parents can be sponsored. DLD ›
  • Fees about AED 9,884.75 in total for the investor, 7 to 10 business days. DLD ›
  • Apply in person, inside the UAE. A lien stays on the property while the visa is valid. GDRFA ›

Combine properties to reach AED 2M

You don't need one big unit. Two or more properties in your name that add up to AED 2 million also qualify, for example two apartments of AED 1.1M each. Off-plan, ready and mortgaged units can be mixed if each meets the rules above.

Golden Visa questions

Does off-plan count?

Yes, it can. The Ministry of Economy and Tourism lists off-plan units worth AED 2M or more in total, bought from local developers approved by the authority. Before you pay, check the project is registered with DLD and the escrow bank: the free Off-Plan Stress Test does this for you.

Can I use a mortgage?

Yes. DLD accepts a mortgaged property with a no-objection letter from your bank showing the amount paid and the balance. The property value still has to be AED 2M or more.

Can my family come?

Yes. DLD says you can sponsor your husband or wife, children and parents. Each family visa has its own fees, listed on the DLD service page.

How long does it take?

DLD lists 7 to 10 business days once your documents are complete. You must be inside the UAE and attend in person. Fees are about AED 9,884.75 in total for the investor (medical test, Emirates ID, residence permit, DLD and admin fees).

What if I sell?

GDRFA places a lien on the property so you keep it while the visa is valid. If you sell and no longer own AED 2M of property, you stop meeting the condition and the visa can be cancelled. Talk to the authorities before you sell.

Is it renewable?

Yes. DLD and GDRFA describe a 10-year residence that can be renewed as long as you still meet the conditions.
