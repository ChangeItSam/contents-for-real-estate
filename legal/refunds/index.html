Cancellation & Refund Policy

SamSurya Realty (samsuryarealty.com) and DealPro Agent (dealproagent.com) · Last updated 25 November 2026

This policy applies to paid plans on samsuryarealty.com (Premium) and dealproagent.com (Agent Pro, Agent Unlimited and top-ups).

1. Plans don't renew automatically

Every plan runs for one month from activation and ends on its own. There is nothing to cancel to avoid being charged again: if you don't pay for the next month, nothing more is taken.

2. Cancelling

You can stop using a plan at any time. Your access continues until the end date of the month you paid for. To close your account and delete your details, message us on WhatsApp (see below).

3. Refunds

  • Changed your mind: refund if you ask within 48 hours of payment and have not run any full report or project in that paid month. The payment processing fee charged by Ziina (about 1.5% to 2% of the payment) is not returned to us when a payment is refunded, so it is deducted from change-of-mind refunds.
  • After that, or once you have used the plan, the current month is not refundable, because the reports and data have already been provided.
  • Top-ups (AED 19 per project) are not refundable once the project has been used.
  • Paid twice or charged the wrong amount: the extra amount is always refunded in full, with no fee deducted.
  • Service problem: if a technical fault on our side stops you using a paid plan for more than 3 days in a row and we can't fix it, we refund the unused part of the month (no fee deducted) or extend your plan, as you prefer.
  • Account closed for misuse (see the Terms): no refund.

4. How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method through Ziina. We start the refund within 5 working days of approving it; the time for it to appear depends on your bank or card provider (usually up to 14 days).

5. How to ask

Send us your name, the email or WhatsApp number on the account, the payment date and the reason.

WhatsApp: +971 52 872 8248 (messages answered on working days, usually within 24 hours), or write to us through the contact & complaints form (reply by email within 3 working days).

This policy doesn't affect any rights you have under UAE consumer protection law.
