This policy explains what personal data we collect through samsuryarealty.com and dealproagent.com, why, and your rights under the UAE Personal Data Protection Law (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021).

1. Who is responsible

SURYA TAMANG Marketing Management (Trader Licence no. 1661461, issued by the Department of Economy and Tourism, Dubai), owned by Surya Narayan Tamang ("Sam Surya").

WhatsApp: +971 52 872 8248 (messages answered on working days, usually within 24 hours), or write to us through the contact & complaints form (reply by email within 3 working days).

2. What we collect

Buyers using the Stress Test, Picks or Premium

  • Your name, WhatsApp number and whether you are a buyer or an agent, when you ask for your result or report.
  • The project you check, your answers and your score, and optional details you type (price, size, payment plan, area, rent).

Agents using DealPro Agent

  • Account details: name, agency, BRN, WhatsApp number, email address and photo.
  • Your password is never stored as typed: we keep only a salted, one-way scrambled version.
  • Usage: the projects you run reports on, the month, and the client name you choose to add (optional).
  • Payments: the plan, the date and the receipt you send us. Card details are handled by Ziina and never reach us.

On your device

The sites save a few settings in your browser's local storage (for example your login session, recent reports and form entries) so you don't have to retype them. We don't use advertising cookies or trackers.

3. Why we use it

  • To give you your result, report or account, and to count plan usage (this is needed to provide the service you asked for).
  • To send you your result and, if you agree, property updates on WhatsApp. Reply STOP at any time to stop updates.
  • To verify agents' email addresses, reset passwords and activate paid plans.
  • To show agents' name, BRN, photo and number on the reports they send to their clients.
  • To keep the Services secure and to meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

We do not sell personal data.

4. Who sees it

  • Your agent's clients: reports an agent sends show that agent's name, agency, BRN, photo and number. Free-trial reports show Sam Surya's details instead.
  • Service providers that run the Services for us: Cloudflare (website hosting), Google (Google Sheets and Apps Script where submissions and accounts are stored, and Gmail for account emails), Ziina (payments), WhatsApp (messages you start), and OpenStreetMap / Leaflet map services when you open a map. Some of these providers process data outside the UAE under their own safeguards.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

5. How long we keep it

  • Buyer submissions: up to 24 months, then deleted.
  • Agent accounts: while the account is active and up to 12 months after the last plan ends; payment and invoice records for as long as tax law requires.

6. Your rights

You can ask us to show you the data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, stop using it for updates, or give you a copy. Message us on WhatsApp from the number you used. We reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the UAE Data Office.

7. Security

Data is stored in access-controlled accounts, passwords are stored only in scrambled form, and the sites use HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you, we will tell you as the law requires.

8. Children

The Services are for adults. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

9. Changes

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version.
