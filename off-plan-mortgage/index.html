🏦 Off-plan mortgage

Buy off-plan in Dubai without paying all cash

Good news: banks in the UAE can finance off-plan homes. The Central Bank sets one key limit: on a property bought off-plan, a bank can lend at most 50% of its value. Here is how off-plan mortgages work, what they cost and how to plan your payment plan around them.

  • Off-plan: max 50% loan-to-value, for every buyer and every price. CBUAE ›
  • Ready first home, non-UAE national: up to 80% under AED 5M, 70% above AED 5M. CBUAE ›
  • Second or investment home: up to 60%, whatever the value. CBUAE ›
  • All your loan repayments together can't exceed 50% of your gross income. CBUAE ›
  • Longest term: 25 years. The bank sets the maximum age at the last payment. CBUAE ›
  • DLD mortgage registration: 0.25% of the loan amount, plus small fees. DLD ›

Last checked 24 Nov 2026 on the UAE Central Bank Rulebook (Regulations Regarding Mortgage Loans, Notice 31/2013, consolidated 2020) and the Dubai Land Department service page. These are maximums: each bank can lend less.

What the reel saidThe short version, in 20 seconds

You don't need all cash to buy off-plan in Dubai. A bank can finance part of it, but the UAE Central Bank caps an off-plan loan at 50% of the property value. Once a home is ready, a non-UAE national buying a first home under AED 5M can borrow up to 80%. Your repayments can't pass half your income, the longest term is 25 years, and Dubai Land Department charges 0.25% of the loan to register the mortgage. The smart move: match your payment plan to what a bank will really lend you, before you book.

Each borrower can claim only one property as a first home. The Central Bank tells banks to be more conservative where the risk is higher, so your offer can be lower.

How an off-plan mortgage works, step by step

  1. Know your budget first. The Central Bank caps your total debt repayments (mortgage, car loan, cards) at 50% of your gross salary and other regular income. Banks also stress-test the rate 2 to 4 points higher, and for an investment property they knock off at least two months' rent. For expats the loan is also capped at 7 times annual income.
  2. Get a pre-approval from a UAE bank. Each bank sets its own eligibility, including rules for non-residents and the maximum age at the last repayment. Ask which developers and projects it finances.
  3. Check the project. Make sure it is registered with Dubai Land Department and your payments go into the project's escrow account. The free Off-Plan Stress Test checks this for you.
  4. Match the payment plan to the loan. During construction a bank can finance at most 50% of the value, so plan to pay at least half from your own money. Look at how much the plan asks for before handover and how much at handover, and how you will pay each part.
  5. Sign and register the mortgage. The bank registers it with DLD online, or you go to a Real Estate Registration Trustee centre. For an off-plan (Oqood) property you need the developer's e-NOC through the Dubai REST app. DLD lists 20 to 25 minutes once the documents are ready.
  6. At handover, the provisional (Oqood) registration becomes a title deed. Keep your bank and developer in the loop well before completion so the handover payment is ready on time.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Expecting 80% on off-plan. The 80% applies to ready first homes. Off-plan is capped at 50%.
  • Booking first, asking the bank later. Get a pre-approval before you pay the booking amount.
  • Forgetting the fees. Budget for 0.25% DLD mortgage registration, trustee fees and the bank's own fees.
  • Counting all the rent. For an investment property banks deduct at least two months' rent when they check what you can afford.
  • Ignoring the project check. A bank loan doesn't replace your own checks on DLD registration and escrow.

Off-plan mortgage questions

Can I get a mortgage on an off-plan property in Dubai?

Yes, banks can finance off-plan. Under the UAE Central Bank rules the loan can be at most 50% of the property value, whatever the price, your nationality or your purpose. Each bank decides which projects it finances.

How much can an expat borrow on a ready home?

For a non-UAE national's first home, up to 80% of the value when it is under AED 5M and 70% above AED 5M. For a second or investment home, up to 60%. These are maximums; the bank can offer less.

Can non-residents get a mortgage?

The Central Bank limits apply to all non-UAE nationals. Whether a bank lends to someone living abroad, and on what terms, is each bank's own policy, so ask the bank directly or message me and I'll point you in the right direction.

How long can the mortgage be?

Up to 25 years under the Central Bank rules. The bank sets the maximum age at the last repayment.

What does it cost to register the mortgage?

Dubai Land Department charges 0.25% of the mortgage value, plus AED 20 in knowledge and innovation fees. A trustee centre charges AED 4,000 + VAT (AED 5,000 + VAT for an off-plan Oqood property). Banks charge their own fees on top.

Does a mortgaged property count for the Golden Visa?

It can. DLD accepts a mortgaged property worth AED 2M or more with a bank letter. See my Golden Visa guide and AED 2M finder.
